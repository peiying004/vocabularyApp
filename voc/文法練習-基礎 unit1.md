# 文法練習-基礎 unit1

## unit 1

| question | correct | distractors | explanation |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| I ___ to school every day. | go | goes,going,gone | 主詞 I 搭配一般現在式時用動詞原形 |
| She ___ a beautiful voice. | has | have,having,had | 第三人稱單數搭配 has，不是 have |
| He ___ his keys yesterday. | lost | loses,losing,losed | lose 的過去式是不規則的 lost |
| You ___ arrive by 9 AM. | must | can,will,may | must 表達必須，義務性最強 |
| The meeting is scheduled ___ Monday. | on | in,at,by | 特定日期／星期前用介系詞 on |
| I ___ this movie three times. | have watched | watched,am watching,will watch | 現在完成式表達到目前為止的經驗次數 |
| If it ___ tomorrow, we'll cancel the trip. | rains | rain,will rain,rained | 第一類條件句 if 子句用現在簡單式 |
| The report ___ by tomorrow. | will be finished | will finish,finishes,finished | 被動語態未來式 will be + 過去分詞 |
| This car is ___ than that one. | more expensive | expensiver,expensivest,most expensive | expensive 為多音節形容詞，比較級用 more |
| She is not ___ tall as her brother. | as | than,then,that | not as X as Y 表達不如 Y 那麼 X |
